Program 01 · Paperwork behind it

The online shop

A great many small online beauty shops import stock and sell it without realising they have become the Responsible Person for it, which carries the duty to hold a safety report and notify before sale. We do not operate that way. Ask us about any product and we will tell you whether we are the Responsible Person or whether the brand is.

  • Per order
  • UK delivery
  • Notified products
  • Ingredients published

Program 02 · Patch tested before colour

Hair services

Colour follows the manufacturer's instruction: a valid allergy alert test, carried out the required interval before the service. We will not compress that for a same-day appointment. We are also honest about whether a requested colour is achievable on hair that has been treated before, rather than starting and finding out.

  • Per service
  • Adults, minors with consent
  • Valid patch test
  • Condition permitting

Program 03 · Within our scope

Beauty treatments

We do not diagnose or treat conditions of the skin or scalp. Where something looks clinical — a lesion, an infection, unexplained hair loss — it goes to a pharmacist, GP or dermatologist, and a treatment may be deferred until it has been looked at. Working on inflamed or broken skin makes problems worse, whatever the booking says.

  • Per service
  • Adults
  • Consultation first
  • Referral if clinical

Program 04 · Ask before you buy

Ingredients and suitability

Ask us before ordering rather than after. Botanical and natural ingredients are among the more common irritants, so natural origin tells you nothing about whether you will react. We will also tell you honestly when a sealed product cannot be returned once opened, because that exception catches people out constantly.
